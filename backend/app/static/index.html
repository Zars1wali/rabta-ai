Why RABTA AI Exists

A missed message.
A lost sale.

"Bhai, yeh suit jo aap ki Instagram story mein tha — woh kya price hai? Mujhe abhi chahiye, delivery ho jayegi?"

That WhatsApp message sat unread for four hours. By the time Ali — a fabric shop owner in Faisalabad — replied, the customer had already bought from someone else. It wasn't Ali's first lost sale that week. He's busy cutting cloth, managing workers, handling accounts. He can't reply to 80 WhatsApp messages a day while running a shop.

Multiply Ali across 5.2 million small businesses in Pakistan — tailors, restaurant owners, mobile shops, shoe wholesalers — all losing customers because they simply can't reply fast enough. That's why we built RABTA AI: an AI employee that knows your products, your prices, your policies, and talks exactly the way you would — so you never lose another sale to a late reply again.
